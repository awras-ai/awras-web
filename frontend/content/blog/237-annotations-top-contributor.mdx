Our most active contributor submitted 237 annotations in a single month. Reading
through them changed how we score every contribution on the platform.

## What we built

The leaderboard used to rank on raw count. We replaced it with a score that
multiplies volume by an agreement factor: how often a contributor's label
matches the consensus of reviewers who saw the same item independently.

## What we measured

Under the new score, the top ten barely moved — the people doing the most work
were also the most careful. What changed is the middle of the table, where
fast-but-noisy submissions no longer outrank slower, cleaner ones.

## What comes next

Agreement is a weak proxy for quality on genuinely ambiguous items, especially
regional vocabulary where the consensus is simply the majority region. We are
adding per-region consensus so a correct Chaoui label stops being penalised.
