Forty hours is not a large corpus by global standards. For Algerian Darija
speech with verified transcripts and explicit consent, it is the largest we know
of that is openly licensed.

## What we built

The protocol is deliberately low-tech: phone microphones, a fixed prompt list,
and a two-pass transcript review. Contributors record where they live, so the
corpus contains cafés, kitchens and traffic instead of studio silence.

## What we measured

Word error rate on a held-out set is 18.4% for our baseline recogniser, and only
2 points worse on the noisiest quartile — evidence that keeping ambient sound
made the models more robust rather than less.

## What comes next

Speaker balance is skewed male, roughly 63/37, and the age distribution thins
out above fifty. The next collection round targets both gaps before we grow the
total hours.
