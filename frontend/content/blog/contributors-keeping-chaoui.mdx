Chaoui is the smallest slice of our corpus and the one most at risk of
disappearing from it. Three annotators from Batna are the reason it has not.

## What we built

We added a household recording guide: consent language in Chaoui and Darija, a
short prompt list built around daily routines, and a submission flow that keeps
elderly speakers anonymous while preserving the regional tag.

## What we measured

The guide turned into 6.2 hours of new speech in five weeks, most of it from
speakers over sixty — a demographic almost absent from web-scraped text and the
one whose vocabulary the model gets wrong most often.

## What comes next

Coverage is still concentrated around Batna. We are looking for contributors in
Khenchela and Oum El Bouaghi so the accent is represented by more than one
valley.
