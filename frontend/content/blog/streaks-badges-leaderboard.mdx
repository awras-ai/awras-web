The contributor profile got its first real update since launch: streaks, badges,
and a leaderboard that finally reflects careful work instead of raw volume.

## What we built

Streaks count days with at least one reviewed contribution and survive a single
missed day. Badges mark milestones that are hard to farm — regional coverage,
review agreement, and long-form audio — and each links to the work that earned
it.

## What we measured

Weekly returning contributors rose 24% in the two weeks after launch, and the
share of sessions with more than five annotations rose as well, so the streak is
pulling depth and not only logins.

## What comes next

Badges are still global. Per-region and per-task badges are next, so someone
holding the line on a small accent gets visible credit for it.
