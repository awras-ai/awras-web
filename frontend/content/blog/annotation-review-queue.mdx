Every annotation platform eventually has the same argument: two contributors
disagree, and the last one to hit save wins. Ours no longer works that way.

## What we built

Conflicting submissions now open a review item instead of replacing each other.
Items are scored by how far apart the annotations are and routed to a reviewer
who has worked on that dialect before.

## What we measured

Silent overwrites dropped from roughly 340 a week to zero, and the median review
item is resolved in under nine hours. Reviewer notifications fell by a third,
because near-identical submissions no longer generate a conflict at all.

## What comes next

Routing still leans on a handful of experienced reviewers. The next iteration
weights reviewer load alongside dialect familiarity so the queue does not
concentrate on the same five people.
