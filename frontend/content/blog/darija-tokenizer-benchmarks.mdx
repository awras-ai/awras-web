A tokenizer trained mostly on English and Modern Standard Arabic treats Darija
the way it treats a typo: it splits it until the pieces look familiar.

## What we built

We benchmarked four public tokenizers against a 12,000-sentence sample of our
corpus, half in Arabic script and half in Latin-script Arabizi, then trained a
byte-level replacement on the same data.

## What we measured

Fertility — tokens produced per word — ran between 3.1 and 4.6 on the public
tokenizers, against 1.7 for ours. Arabizi was the worst case everywhere: numeral
substitutions like 3, 7 and 9 fragment almost every word they appear in.

## What comes next

Lower fertility means more sentence per context window and cheaper inference,
but it also means retraining every downstream checkpoint. We are publishing the
vocabulary first and the migrated models once the evaluations line up.
