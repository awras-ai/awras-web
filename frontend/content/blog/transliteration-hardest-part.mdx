A single Algerian sentence can carry Arabic script, Arabizi digits and a French
loanword spelled three different ways. Tokenizers hate it, and so do our
evaluation metrics.

## What we built

We shipped a normalisation layer that maps Arabizi to Arabic script before
training, while keeping the original surface form as metadata. The model sees a
consistent script; the dataset keeps the evidence of how people actually write.

## What we measured

Normalisation lifted retrieval accuracy on our internal benchmark by 11 points,
but it also erased spelling signals that identify a writer's region. We now
report both scores, normalised and raw, so the trade-off stays visible.

## What comes next

The mapping is still rule-based and breaks on names and brand words. A learned
transliterator trained on annotator-confirmed pairs is the next milestone, and
it needs roughly 50k more aligned examples.
