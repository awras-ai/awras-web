Annotation studio 1.3 is out. The theme of this release is simple: you should
never have to reach for the mouse to review an item.

## What we built

Every review action now has a shortcut — approve, reject, flag, and skip on the
home row, plus scrubbing and playback-speed controls for audio items. Batch
approve lets reviewers clear a filtered queue in one confirmation.

## What we measured

In the beta cohort, median time per reviewed item dropped from 21 to 9 seconds,
and the rejection rate held steady, which suggests the speed came from the
interface rather than from less careful reading.

## What comes next

The queue still refetches on every action, which shows on slow connections.
Optimistic updates and an offline draft mode are queued for 1.4.
