Today we are releasing awras-chat-v0, our first conversational model that
replies in Algerian Darija instead of falling back to Modern Standard Arabic or
French. It is small, it is opinionated, and it exists because contributors
annotated 1.2M pairs over eight months.

## What we built

The base is a 7B open-weights model, continued-pretrained on 4.1B tokens of
Algerian web text and transcribed speech, then instruction-tuned on the
community pairs. Every pair carries the annotator's region so we can weight
dialects instead of averaging them into a flat register nobody speaks.

## What we measured

We evaluated on five regional accents — Algérois, Oranais, Constantinois,
Chaoui and Kabyle-influenced Darija — with native speakers scoring fluency and
faithfulness blind. Fluency lands above 4.1/5 everywhere except Chaoui, where
we are still short on data.

## What comes next

Code-switching mid-sentence remains the biggest failure mode, and transliteration
between Arabizi and Arabic script is unreliable in long answers. Both are data
problems before they are modelling problems, which is where the annotation
queue goes next.
